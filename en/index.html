(02) THE STUDIO

AT LÚDICA, WE DESIGN TRANSFORMATIVE IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCES THAT MERGE ART, CREATIVITY, AND TECHNOLOGY TO TELL STORIES WITH LASTING IMPACT. WE TURN CONVENTIONAL SPACES INTO LIVING ENVIRONMENTS THAT SPARK EMOTIONS AND INSPIRE CULTURAL CONNECTION.

— FOUNDED BY ALEJANDRO STRAUSS & MYKAELA PLOTKIN

WE COLLABORATE WITH MUSEUMS, CULTURAL INSTITUTIONS, CITY GOVERNMENTS, HOTELS, GLOBAL BRANDS, AND SPORTS ORGANIZATIONS TO DEVELOP IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCES THAT RESONATE DEEPLY. OUR FLAGSHIP PROJECT, MUSEO DE LAS IDENTIDADES DE JUÁREZ, EXEMPLIFIES OUR DISTINCTIVE APPROACH — INTEGRATING STORYTELLING, ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY, AND CULTURAL INNOVATION. EACH PROJECT IS CONCEIVED TO FORGE AUTHENTIC CONNECTIONS BETWEEN AUDIENCES, SPACES, AND NARRATIVES, DELIVERING MEMORABLE ACTIVATIONS AND INTERACTIVE ENVIRONMENTS DESIGNED FOR LONG-TERM RELEVANCE.

(04) PROJECTS

SELECTED PROJECTS

P.01MUSEOGRAPHY / GOVERNMENT — CIUDAD JUÁREZ

MUSEO IDENTIDADES JUÁREZ

Full creative direction and production. History, culture and immersive technology integrated: holograms, interactive installations and 360° experiences. Since opening, it has become the cultural destination of Ciudad Juárez.

  • 360° digital experiences
  • Permanent full installation
P.02INSTALLATION / PUBLIC SPACE — MEXICO CITY METRO

GALERÍA METRO BELLAS ARTES

Motion sensors and generative art turned the busiest corridor in the city into a living digital canvas for the Metro's 50th anniversary. The station went from infrastructure to destination.

  • +6M people reached
  • 60 days on display
  • 100% earned media
P.03NIGHT JOURNEY — DAY OF THE DEAD

CELEBRANDO LA ETERNIDAD

Chapultepec Forest — the most visited park in Latin America — transformed into a nighttime immersive experience. Technology, tradition and territory. A free event, sold out every single night.

  • Chapultepec largest park in LATAM
  • Sold-out every night
P.04URBAN INTERVENTION — MEXICO CITY / FIFA 2026

SANTA ÚRSULA COAPA

A mural trail of 30+ permanent interventions around Estadio Banorte, created hand in hand with neighbors, families and local storytellers. The memory of a territory on the eve of the FIFA World Cup 2026.

  • +30 permanent interventions
  • Mexico City Gov. Tourism Ministry
P.05MUSEOGRAPHY / SPORTS

MUFU — MUSEO DEL FUTBOL

Sports heritage meets cutting-edge technology: hologram zones, 360° highlights, game-style challenges, large-scale projection, immersive audio and a themed sport bar. The passion of football, turned into experience.

  • Holograms & 360° highlights
  • Themed sport bar
